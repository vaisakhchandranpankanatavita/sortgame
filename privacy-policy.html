01

What we collect

Sort Escape does not ask you to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email, or any other personal information to play. We — the developer — do not operate any server that receives your gameplay data.

The game itself only reads and writes local, on-device data: your level progress, coin/gem/star balances, unlocked themes, achievement status, and settings (sound, music, haptics). None of that is transmitted anywhere by us.

02

What stays on your device

All progress is stored in a single local save file on your device using Android's standard app-private storage. It is sandboxed to Sort Escape — no other app can read it, and we have no remote copy of it.

03

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sort Escape shows optional rewarded ads (for example, "watch an ad for +5 moves") and, less frequently, interstitial ads between levels, served through Google AdMob. You are never required to watch an ad to keep playing — it's always an optional way to get a bonus.

To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK running inside the app can collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, coarse location (e.g. country/region), and ad interaction data. We do not receive this data directly — it's collected by Google under its own privacy terms, linked below.

Google's own policies govern how that data is used: Google Privacy Policy  ·  Google's EU User Consent Policy

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android versions), or by installing the Google-provided controls for your Google account.

05

Children's privacy

Sort Escape is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will address it.

06

Data retention & deletion

Because your progress lives only on your device, you're always in control of it:

  • Clear it any time — Android Settings → Apps → Sort Escape → Storage → Clear data.
  • Delete it entirely — uninstalling the app removes the local save file along with it.

We hold no copy of it to delete on our side, because we never received one.

07

Security

The app requests only the Android permissions it needs to function: vibration (for haptic feedback) and internet/network-state access (required for ads to load). Your local save data is sandboxed by Android's app-storage model, the same protection every installed app relies on.

08

Changes to this policy

If we add new features that change what data is collected — an account system, cloud save, analytics, or a leaderboard, for instance — we'll update this page and change the "Effective" date at the top before that feature ships. We won't retroactively expand data collection without saying so here first.
